# The Biggest Rule: Don't Stuff Him Full of Food

This sounds completely backwards.

He's starving.

Obviously he needs food.

Every normal instinct tells you:

> **FEED THE POOR BASTARD.**

Please don't let him gorge.

A severely starved body can react very badly when suddenly given too much food.

There's something called **refeeding syndrome.**

You don't need to understand all the science behind it.

The simple version is:

> **His body has adapted to surviving without enough food. If you suddenly dump a huge amount of nutrition into him, his body may not be able to handle the sudden change, and he can become seriously sick.**

That's the fucked-up paradox here:

**A starving dog needs food.**

**A starving dog can also be hurt by being fed incorrectly.**

So we're careful.
